Podcast Studio

A private production and publishing tool for the YouTube channel GLOBE TALKERS.

Podcast Studio is a desktop application that runs locally on the computer of the channel owner, Nicolas Neveu. It is used to produce English-learning podcast episodes and to publish them on the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not hosted online, not distributed and not sold, and it has a single user: the channel owner.

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads a finished episode video to the owner's channel (YouTube Data API, videos.insert).
  • Sets the title, description, tags, category, language and privacy status or scheduled publication time.
  • Sets the custom thumbnail (thumbnails.set) and uploads English captions (captions.insert).
  • Adds the video to one of the owner's playlists (playlistItems.insert, playlists.list).

Access is granted by the channel owner with Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0) and can be revoked at any time.
